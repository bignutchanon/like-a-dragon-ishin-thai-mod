  … by the name of Thomas Glover. **He** is a weapons merchant from the British Empire, and **he** is
  a downright fearsome individual… **he** is of stern disposition"*

- msg `uid006f0040#217` "How is it that **he** has so many weapons" + `#220` 「悲しい**男**だな」
