## ประวัติย่อจากแผนผังในเกม (ใช้ตั้งโทนและตรวจความสัมพันธ์)

### Sakamoto Ryoma (Ryoma)

> Tosa Domain goshi.
> 
> Recently completed his sword training in Edo.
> 
> He has returned to Tosa for the first time in a year. 

### Hijikata Toshizo (Hijikata)

> Shinsengumi Deputy Chief.
> 
> Witnessed Ryoma's entry duel with Captain Nagakura and clash with Captain Okita.
> 
> He has summoned Ryoma to a local ryotei.

### Otose (Otose)

> Okami of Teradaya, one of the largest inns in Kyo.
> 
> Despite her very stated financial interests, she is rather lenient with Ryoma's tab at the inn.

### Takechi Hanpeita (Takechi)

> Tosa Domain high goshi and Ryoma's sworn brother.
> 
> As an older brother figure to Ryoma, the two grew up together under the care of Yoshida Toyo.
> 
> He is also the founder of the Tosa Loyalist Party, which intends to end the class system in Tosa.

### Oryo (Oryo)

> Maid at Teradaya, one of the largest inns in Kyo.
> 
> Has a rather low opinion of Ryoma, who lives as a layabout ronin with a growing tab while lodging at her workplace.

### Okita Soji (Okita)

> Shinsengumi Captain, First Division.
> 
> Considered the strongest member of the Shinsengumi, who are already viewed as the strongest men in Kyo.
> 
> After witnessing Ryoma's entry duel with Captain Nagakura, he takes a keen interest in the new member.

### Kondo Isami (Kondo)

> Shinsengumi Chief and heir of the Tennen Rishin style.
> 
> He crosses swords at Asahi with Ryoma, who seeks the identity of the masked man.

### Todo Heisuke (Todo)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Eighth Division.
> 
> The youngest Captain among the officers.
> 
> He offers to show Ryoma the ins and outs of serving in the Shinsengumi.

### Nagakura Shinpachi (Nagakura)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Second Division.
> 
> Administers Ryoma's entry trial at the Shinsengumi tryouts.

### Harada Sanosuke (Harada)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Tenth Division.
> 
> His crass demeanor gets him in trouble with senior member Captain Inoue.

### Katsura Kogoro (Katsura)

> Leader of the Choshu Domain.
> 
> Revealed his true name to Ryoma at Ikedaya.
> 
> He hints to Ryoma that he is acquainted with Takechi Hanpeita, he convinces Ryoma to fake a duel and let him escape.

### Ito Kashitaro (Ito)

> Shinsengumi Advisor.
> 
> Strives to expand his powers within the group as Chief Kondo remains absent.

### Inoue Genzaburo (Inoue)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Sixth Division.
> 
> The oldest and most experienced veteran among the officers.

### Takeda Kanryusai (Takeda)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Fifth Division.
> 
> Trained in Koshu military tactics, which he teaches and utilizes within his division.

### Yoshida Toyo (Toyo)

> Tosa Domain Magistrate.
> 
> Raised an orphaned Ryoma and saved him from being executed in Tosa.
> 
> He arranges to meet with Ryoma at Kengoshi Schoolhouse.

### Okada Izo (Izo)

> Tosa Loyalist Party Member.
> 
> Displeased with the recent Party Representative selection, he challenges Ryoma to a fight.

### Saigo Kichinosuke (Saigo)

> A large man who dueled Ryoma in the buff at the bathhouse.
> 
> He later reveals himself to be Satsuma Army Commander Saigo Kichinosuke.

### Yamazaki Susumu (Yamazaki)

> Shinsengumi Inspector.
> 
> Waits for Ryoma while dressed as a vagrant, then informs him of a Shinsengumi deserter who must face punishment.

### Matsubara Chuji (Matsubara)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Fourth Division.
> 
> A recently-appointed captain with a strait-laced attitude. He is disgusted by the selfish behavior of some of his peers.
> 
> Since he put great effort into acquiring his position, he expresses displeasure with Ryoma's abrupt appointment to Captain.

### Nakaoka Shintaro (Nakaoka)

> Former Tosan ronin, self-exiled.
> 
> One of the few people present for Yoshida Toyo's murder. Initially suspected Takechi of the crime.
> 
> He asks Ryoma to meet him at Kiyomizu Temple, where the two are ambushed by Tosa Loyalist Party members.

### Yamauchi Yodo (Yodo)

> Lord of the Tosa Domain.
> 
> Used Takechi Hanpeita in an attempt to essentially sell Japan off to Great Britain wholesale. Aimed to become Emperor of Japan so he could turn the nation into a British territory.

### Sasaki Tadasaburo (Sasaki)

> Mimawarigumi Officer.
> 
> Maintains order within the city of Kyo.

### Tokugawa Yoshinobu (Tokugawa)

> 15th Shogun of the Edo Bakufu.
> 
> A smart, calculating young man tasked with steering the nation through a difficult situation.
> 
> Confronts Ryoma about the intentions behind storming Edo Castle, questioning the man's resolve.

### Suzuki Mikisaburo (Suzuki)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Ninth Division.
> 
> Said to have been handpicked for his position by Shinsengumi Advisor Ito Kashitaro. His opinions and actions regularly align with Ito's own.

### Tani Sanjuro (Tani)

> Shinsengumi Captain, Seventh Division.
> 
> Known for abusing his position as Captain for personal gain.

### The Real Okita Soji (Real Okita)

> Former member of the Miburoshigumi, predecessor to the Shinsengumi.
> 
> He plotted to murder Serizawa Kamo to solve a power struggle within the group, but was killed in the fray instead.
> 
> One of his killers, Hirayama Goro, takes up his identity after his death.

### Katsu Rintaro (Katsu)

> Bakufu Navy Admiral.
> 
> Urges Saigo Kichinosuke of Satsuma to declare war against the Bakufu, despite being one of the military's leaders. Introduces Saigo to a man named Sakamoto Ryoma, who has ties to a British arms dealer.

### Niibori Matsusuke (Niibori)

> A Choshu samurai.
> 
> Admitted to Ryoma that "Niibori Matsusuke" is a fake name.
> 
> After witnessing Ryoma's skills, he invites Ryoma to the Choshu Domain, but gets turned down. He then provides the name of a capable informant: the Bathkeeper of Sai.

### Ikumatsu (Ikumatsu)

> Barmaid running the izakaya owned by Katsura Kogoro.
> 
> Gets exasperated when men show up in the middle of the night to start brawls at her bar.

### Man in White (Man in White)

> A mysterious man clad in white.
> 
> Fights Ryoma to the death, as ordered by Deputy Chief Hijikata.

### The Impostor Ryoma (The Impostor Ryoma)

> A mysterious individual who has been using the name Sakamoto Ryoma.
> 
> He attends a secret meeting with Satsuma Army Commander Saigo Kichinosuke and Bakufu Navy Admiral Katsu Rintaro.

### The Masked Man (The Masked Man)

> The man who murdered Yoshida Toyo at Kochi Castle.
> 
> After trading blows with Ryoma, he vanishes into the night.

### The Real Inoue Genzaburo (Real Inoue)

> Former member of the Miburoshigumi, predecessor to the Shinsengumi.
> 
> He plotted to murder Serizawa Kamo to solve a power struggle within the group, but was killed in the fray instead.
> 
> One of his killers, Serizawa Kamo, takes up his identity after his death.

### The Real Nagakura Shinpachi (Real Nagakura)

> Former member of the Miburoshigumi, predecessor to the Shinsengumi.
> 
> He plotted to murder Serizawa Kamo to solve a power struggle within the group, but was killed in the fray instead.
> 
> One of his killers, Hirama Jusuke, takes up his identity after his death.

### Yamanami Keisuke (Yamanami)

> Former Shinsengumi General Secretary and Third Division Captain.
> 
> Attempted to desert the Shinsengumi, but was found and captured. He was told he would only be allowed to live if he could kill Ryoma in a duel.
> 
> After Ryoma refuses to execute him, Deputy Chief Hijikata performs the deed.
